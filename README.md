Apples Game:

A simple yet challenging 2D platformer where the player navigates through a dangerous level,collecting apples and avoiding deadly holes to reach the end. 

Gameplay and Objective:

Explore a level filled with tricky platforms Collect all Apples to maximize your score Avoid falling into holes Reach the end point safely
Control Strategies:
W / Up Arrow : Move Forward
S/ Down Arrow : Go Backwards
A/ Left Arrow: Turn Left 
D / Right Arrow Turn Right
Space : Jump

Characteristics:

Responsive Platforming Controls: Smooth and precise movement with consistent jump mechanics for an enjoyable gameplay experience.
Collectible System: Gather apples scattered throughout the level to increase your score while encouraging exploration.
Clean Level Design: Straightforward platform layouts focused on learning movement mechanics and improving
Built With :

Engine: Godot Engine 
Language: GD Script

Background & Motivation:

The game was created as a real world application for learning how to make 2D platformers using Godot Engine. The most important things that were learned are how to create responsive player controls, collecting elements mechanics, and overall game mechanics.

Future Improvements:

Checkpoints: Respawn at checkpoints.
